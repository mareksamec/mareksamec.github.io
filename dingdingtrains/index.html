Ding Ding Trains

A gentle train game for young children.

Swipe to drive, stop at stations, pick up passengers and cargo, and take them where they are wanted. At every level crossing the lights flash, the bells ring, the gates come down, and the cars wait while your train rolls by.

  • No reading needed. Every task is shown with pictures.
  • No crashes, no timers, no game over, no lost points.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts.
  • Plays fully offline and collects no data.
